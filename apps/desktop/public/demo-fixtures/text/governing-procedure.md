**Governing procedure — evidence required before a review note**
Source: maintenance_sop_unit4.pdf — "Unit 4 Maintenance Review Procedure", revision SD-01, owner: Asset Integrity Cell

This procedure governs the evidence required before a Unit 4 management-review note can be drafted.

- **Required evidence** — identify the affected asset, cite the source page or drawing region, preserve confidence and clearance, retrieve the applicable procedure reference, and record any unresolved ambiguity.
- **Acceptance rule** — a finding may be included in a review draft once it has a source location and an identified asset. A low-confidence tag or connection must stay labelled as unresolved and requires human confirmation.
- **Escalation** — a high-priority corrosion observation requires maintenance engineering review.
- **Authority boundary** — this procedure does not authorize field work, plant isolation, repair, or equipment operation.
- **Deliverable requirement** — the approval note must contain source-backed findings, affected assets, deterministic totals, review status, and a statement that final organizational approval remains with an authorized person.

Applied to this review: F-01, F-02, and F-03 each carry a source region and an identified asset, so each is admissible. F-02 remains labelled low-confidence per the acceptance rule.
